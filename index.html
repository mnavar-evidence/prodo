AFTER THE UPLOAD

Submitted is one thing.
Showing up is another.

When your team updates packaging, how do you confirm the intended image is actually live—your own checks, an agency, or a monitoring tool?

We want to understand what already works, and where checking and follow-up still take effort.

  1. 01

    Establish what should be live

    Map the intended image to the right product, including when the new packaging applies and whether both versions are allowed.

  2. 02

    Make differences easy to review

    Bring the reference, observed image, source, and observation time into a clear comparison.

  3. 03

    Check again after a correction

    Record what is observed after the brand or agency updates the listing. Don’t confuse submission with confirmation.

A proposed workflow, not a claim of proven capabilities. Our goal is a trustworthy result with simpler setup and lower total cost.

Charcoal label, version 1.
Sample observation: 17 September 2026.
Finding
The illustrated listing image differs from the supplied reference after the stated transition period.
Next step
Confirm the product mapping and any exception. If an update is needed, the brand or agency submits it; then recheck.
What would support this finding?

An ASIN-mapped approved asset, its effective dates, the source URL, an observation timestamp, and file hashes. Missing information would be flagged—not treated as a successful check.

Matching an approved image would not establish regulatory compliance or physical product authenticity.
